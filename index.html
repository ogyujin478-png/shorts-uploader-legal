Terms of Service

What this app is

Shorts Uploader is a personal, single-user tool. It connects to the TikTok Content Posting API so its owner can send their own short-form product videos to their own TikTok account as private drafts, which the owner then reviews and publishes themselves from the TikTok app.

Who can use it

This app is operated by one individual for their own TikTok account. It is not a public service, is not offered to other users, and does not accept sign-ups from anyone else.

What you may upload

  • Only video content the owner has the rights to publish.
  • No content that violates TikTok's Community Guidelines or Terms of Service.

No warranty

This app is provided as-is, with no guarantee that uploads will succeed or that TikTok's API will remain available in its current form. The owner is responsible for reviewing every draft before it is published.

Changes

These terms may be updated as the app changes. Continued use after an update means the owner accepts the revised terms.

Privacy Policy

Who this covers

This app has exactly one user: its owner. It does not collect data from, or make decisions about, anyone else.

What is stored, and where

  • Video files and captions the owner supplies, stored on the owner's own computer or private server.
  • TikTok access and refresh tokens, obtained through TikTok's official OAuth login, stored locally so the app can call the API on the owner's behalf.
  • Nothing here is uploaded to any server operated by anyone other than TikTok and, temporarily, a private cloud storage bucket the owner controls.

Who this data is shared with

Only TikTok, through its official Content Posting API, to create the draft post. This app does not sell, share, or otherwise transfer any data to advertisers or other third parties.

How long it is kept

Video files are kept locally by the owner. TikTok tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the account or revokes access from TikTok's own settings. Temporary cloud copies used to prepare an upload are deleted automatically once that upload finishes.

Deleting your data

Because the owner is the only user, the owner can delete all local data, including stored tokens, at any time by removing the app's local files, and can revoke TikTok access at any time from the TikTok app's connected-apps settings.

Shorts Uploader is a personal automation tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by TikTok.
